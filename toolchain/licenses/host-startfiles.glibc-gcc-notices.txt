Host start-up objects linked into every x86-64 ELF in the tarball: licence notices
==================================================================================

Not an upstream file. Assembled from the leading comment blocks of the upstream sources of
the objects that Ubuntu's clang 18 links into each executable and shared library
(Scrt1.o, crti.o, crtn.o from glibc; crtbeginS.o, crtendS.o from GCC's libgcc).
glibc: https://github.com/bminor/glibc (mirror of sourceware.org/git/glibc.git) tag glibc-2.39
GCC:   https://github.com/gcc-mirror/gcc (mirror of gcc.gnu.org/git/gcc.git) tag releases/gcc-13.3.0
